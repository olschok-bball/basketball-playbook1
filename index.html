📘 Mobile Basketball Playbook

Play 1: 14 Back

Replaces “Center Dive”.

Quick action flowing into a back option from 1–4 alignment; use to punish top lock/overplays.

Play 2: Side Horns

Horns variant with strong-side emphasis and weak-side options to occupy help.

Play 3: Center Backscreen

Center sets the backscreen for a guard/wing cutting middle or weak side; great vs. switches or overplays.

Coach’s Note: Don’t force the backdoor pass! If it isn’t possible, the point guard runs the second cut. If that’s still not open, the centers flow into a pick-and-roll.

Play 4: Elevator

Elevator doors for a shooter; set up with decoy action to disguise the gate close.
